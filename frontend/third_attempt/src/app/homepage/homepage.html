Precaution: If this reaches you (Jolie), then this is my reflection, try not to judge it.

There are a few things I wanted to clear out. Since I know you don't visit this anymore, this is more like a self-reflection for me. But if you do, this is worth a read :)

Recall back to the day you sent the message on Discord. At first, when I received it, I shaked a little, I didn't genuinely feel "pain" at first, but it got me thinking for the night. I kept asking 'why?' or asked 'what was I missing?' I couldn't really wrap my head around the message. This was the first stage, eventually I knocked myself to sleep for a few hours. This was when I was in a "Denial" Stage.

The next morning is when it started to hurt, a lot. I missed finding new songs in your "emo playlist", seeing that it's gone. Despite that, I still put that aside and went for a jog. The jog works well, it clears my head, makes me thinks straight. With that energy, I continue my day. But if you ask me if it actually hurt ?

Mhm, it does. Even though you're always in the back of my mind, even though i always found myself checking if the "emo playlist" maybe would be back. I gaslight myself quite a lot,

"Maybe I'm too fat?"
"Maybe I'm too short?"
"My score is too low, maybe I'm dumb?"
"I express too much, maybe I'm just a weirdo"
"Maybe I'm just not as good as other people"

On the night of that day, I accepted these points, these became the main objectives that I will focus on. Because if there is a next time, i won't lose to anyone again. Admittedly, I was also in the wrong, but I stopped caring about it, I was tired.

I don't care, as long as I work harder, then at least, maybe, the next time I see her, I won't lose to anyone again.

I don't feel anything against her, but rather adoration. I couldn't make myself hate her, forget her, or whatever people these days say it.

How can you bring yourself to hate somebody that you adore so much ? She didn't do anything wrong to begin with, it was me who initiated. But even understanding that.

I guess this is my toxic trait, I stopped caring about the fact that she has a boy. I just didn't want to lose to him. But in a sense, this is good. At least I have a valid reason to improve. There was no hatred. If anything, I wanted to redeem myself. With this mindset, I gaslighted myself.

And it worked.

I do have to admit, my ego is very high and I dislike losing the most. Of course, only in important matters, who would care losing in something that doesn't matter ? But now, it does. I lost to him, and her in the process.

Do I hate him ? Of course not, I don't even know him. Do I feel anything aginst him ? No, I don't care about him. He can be replace with anyone, and I would still keep up this mindset.

But I still haven't answer myself the question that I kept asking myself, and my friends asked me.

Why Jolie?

Now, I will tell you why :)

Her birthday is on 8th of October. I presume her favourite colour is among either Cherry Blossom or Skyblue. Although she did mentioned "Gray" when I gifted her a gift. Jolie and I met in postgraduate school. We were friends for a while. One of the thing I love the most about Jolie is her eyes and smile. She could go all day long and talk, words can easily lie and be twisted. But her eyes can hardly lie, her smile sparks Joy that anyone hardly sees.

Her idea of care is subtle, even though she doesn't say it clearly, and one may not see it. It is in her actions that proves. After all, she was the only person in 4 years, after 3 other people who dated. She was the only person that made me go to the extent I go to. How else could you say it ? She was my Lookout for Wednesday, my Hope for Thursday, and my Joy and Excitement for Friday.

Have I answer your question ? the "me" that reads this ?
"No, not yet, more."
Fineee.

Oh, how could I forgot, I can't ever forget things when it comes to her. The spark in here eyes, the smile that she hid but radiates Joy. When she received that "Gray" bunny, I couldn't help it but to love her. How can I not ? I think on that day, I told myself, "Nah, I don't care anymore, I just want her". I swear I can't never forget that, her reaction, her smile, her laughs, her eyes at that moment. If people read this, you wouldn't get it.

I already lost hope in "real love or true love" or whatever people call it before her. But there she is, standing there, right infront of me for the whole 2 years.

I hope that answers "Why Jolie". And as for now. Yes, she has a boy, I respect her decision fully and I hope she's the very best. But, if there's a next time, I will not lose her, I will not lose to any boy. Until then, my mindset is as is. That is all for this reflection. Obviously there are much more reasons to answer "Why Jolie", but, this is fine :)

If there were to be another chance, I will never lose her. But until that day, or life. Nothing else matter except keep improving.
